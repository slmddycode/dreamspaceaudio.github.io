DreamSpace Rotate

DreamSpace Rotate is a small private tool used only by the owner of the DreamSpaceAudio YouTube channel. On a schedule, it starts and ends the channel's own live broadcasts, so each one is saved as a video on the channel. It is not offered to anyone else.
